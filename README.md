# 🌱 EcoTwin AI

## Overview
EcoTwin AI is a Carbon Footprint Awareness Platform that helps users understand, track, and reduce their carbon emissions through AI-powered insights and recommendations.

## Features
- Carbon Footprint Calculator
- Smart Dashboard
- AI Sustainability Coach
- What-If Simulator

## Live Demo
https://ecotwin-ai-platform-5aov.bolt.host

## Problem Statement
Design a solution that helps individuals understand, track, and reduce their carbon footprint through simple actions and personalized insights.
